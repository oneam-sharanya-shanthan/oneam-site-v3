Built to fund your operations and fuel your growth

OneAM is an American company setting a new industry standard in early pay. We connect SMBs and founders selling receivables with sophisticated institutional investors — a faster, more business-friendly alternative to traditional factoring.

The problem we're solving

SMBs and founders are the backbone of the U.S. economy and the most underserved when it comes to working capital.

Traditional factoring can be slow, painful, and expensive. Bank lines are out of reach for many. The alternatives are too costly to be long-term solutions.

OneAM was built to fix that. We bring modern infrastructure, quantitative risk assessment and a platform that integrates seamlessly with the tools you already use to run your business.
